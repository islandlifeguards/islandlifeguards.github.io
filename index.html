About Island Lifeguards

Rooted in Dix Hills with services across Long Island, our mission is to deliver professional, community-focused lifeguarding with an emphasis on prevention, training, and responsive care.

We hire only certified lifeguards with up-to-date training in lifeguarding, CPR/AED, and first aid. Our team partners with property managers, event planners, and families to build safety plans that reduce risk and improve outcomes. Reliability, accountability, and visible professionalism are at the heart of what we do.
