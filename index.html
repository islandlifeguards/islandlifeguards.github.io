Why Island Lifeguards

Safety You Can Trust. Service You'll Remember.

We bring the same professional standards found at premier aquatic facilities directly to your private event — so you can relax and enjoy every moment.

Certified & Experienced Professionals

Every member of our team holds current American Red Cross or equivalent lifeguard certification. We bring real experience from pools, beaches, and high-attendance events — not just a card.

Reliable & Professionally Vetted

Every guard we place is background-checked, professionally trained, and held to a high standard of conduct. Our clients trust us to send only the best — and we take that responsibility seriously.

Customized Safety Solutions

No two events are the same. We work with you before your event to assess the setting, determine appropriate guard-to-swimmer ratios, and create a safety plan tailored to your needs.

Island Lifeguards

Founded in Dix Hills and rooted in the Long Island community, Island Lifeguards was built on a single belief: every aquatic event deserves professional oversight. Whether you're hosting a summer pool party for thirty guests or managing a waterfront property with continuous traffic, we provide the staffing to keep everyone safe — without disrupting the experience you've worked to create.

We serve residential clients, HOAs, corporate events, private clubs, and everything in between across Nassau and Suffolk County.
