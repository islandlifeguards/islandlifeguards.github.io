Who We Are

About Island Lifeguards

Founded in Dix Hills and rooted in the Long Island community, Island Lifeguards provides certified, professionally trained lifeguard staffing for residential pools, waterfront properties, and private events. Every guard is ARC-certified, background-checked, and experienced in private-event coverage. We serve Nassau and Suffolk County — bringing the same professional standard found at premier aquatic facilities directly to your event.
