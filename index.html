About Island Lifeguards

Founded in Dix Hills and rooted in the Long Island community, Island Lifeguards provides certified, professionally trained Private Lifeguard Coverage for residential pools, waterfront properties, and private events. Every guard is ARC-certified, background-checked, and experienced in private-event coverage. We serve Nassau and Suffolk County — bringing the same professional standard found at premier aquatic facilities directly to your event.

Isaac Saia-Gluck
Founder & Operational Manager

Isaac Saia-Gluck is the Founder and Operational Manager of Island Lifeguards, bringing eight years of lifeguarding and swim instruction experience to every assignment. Certified and trained through the American Red Cross, he has spent years providing private lifeguard coverage for parties and events across Long Island. As an Industrial Engineering major at Hofstra University, Isaac applies a systems-oriented approach to scheduling, safety planning, and operational logistics. Alongside his sister, Mikaela Gluck, he co-leads Island Lifeguards with a focus on reliable coverage, consistent safety standards, and professional service throughout Dix Hills and Long Island.
